First Law: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

First Law is a puzzle game by l1tbo. This policy covers the game on Android and iPhone.

What the game collects: nothing

First Law has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting, and it never sends anything over the internet. It doesn't collect, store or share any personal information, from anyone of any age.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels finished, best move counts, your stats) and your settings are saved on your device only, so you can carry on where you left off. Nobody else can see them, including us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Vibration

The game uses your phone's vibration for haptic feedback. Android grants this to apps automatically, and the iPhone needs no permission for it. It reads nothing from your phone.

App stores

Google Play and the App Store handle downloads and may collect information under their own privacy policies. First Law receives none of it.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example because a future version adds cloud saves, this page will say so before that version is released, with a new date above.
